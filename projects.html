Normal Project

Classic 3BHK Independent House

📍 Location: Madurai, Tamil Nadu
📐 Area: 1800 sq.ft
💰 Budget: ₹45 Lakhs
⏱️ Timeline: 8 Months
📅 Completed: March 2025

Project Details: Standard 3BHK villa for middle-class family. Includes 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, hall, kitchen, and small backyard. Vastu compliant plan with cross ventilation. Used local materials for cost optimization. Client wanted low-maintenance design.

Innovative Project

Eco Farmhouse with Bamboo Architecture

📍 Location: Ooty Hills
📐 Area: 2.5 Acres + 4000 sq.ft built
💰 Budget: ₹1.8 Crores
⏱️ Timeline: 14 Months
📅 Completed: Aug 2025

Project Details: 100% sustainable farmhouse using treated bamboo, mud walls, and recycled wood. Zero concrete structure. Includes organic farm, natural swimming pond, and off-grid solar power. Designed to blend with hillside terrain.

Innovation Highlights: Earthbag foundation, bamboo truss roof, natural insulation, compost toilets, greywater recycling. IGBC Platinum certified. Featured in Architecture Digest India.
